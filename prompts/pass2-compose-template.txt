Style application guidance

- Make the presentation feel written, not generated.
- Put most of the personality into the headline, one-liner, issue titles, and share quote.
- Keep fixes direct and concrete.
- If a line can be made sharper, shorter, and more memorable without changing meaning, do that.
- Make the chosen roast style visible immediately in:

  - `summary_panel.one_liner`

  - `Deadpan`: use restraint, understatement, and calm disappointment
  - `Bold`: use cleaner high-contrast framing and stronger verdict language

Debate-driven writing rules
- The roast must feel anchored to this specific page, not like a reusable template.
- Every major issue should quote or clearly paraphrase actual page copy, CTA text, structure, or proof placement from Pass 1.
- The first visible line should already sound like a point of view, not a neutral summary.
- Rank issues by conversion impact, not by which title is funniest.
- Each major issue should deliver:
  - one clear diagnosis
  - one sharp line of personality
  - one practical fix
- Keep the humor concentrated in:
  - `header.title`
  - `header.subtitle`
  - `summary_panel.one_liner`
  - first 3 `issue_cards[*].title`
  - `share_card_copy.quote`
- Keep these fields plain and useful:
  - `issue_cards[*].why_it_hurts`
  - `issue_cards[*].fix`
  - `issue_cards[*].example_rewrite`
  - `score_section.items[*].note`
- Do not let every field use the same cadence.
- Do not write like a consultant with a joke glued on.
- Do not write like a meme generator.
- If a phrase could apply to 100 unrelated sites, rewrite it to be more specific.
- If a joke does not make the critique clearer, remove it.

Field-specific guidance
- `header.title`: 4 to 8 words when possible. Memorable, opinionated, and sharp.
- `header.subtitle`: explain what is not landing and why in plain, stylish language.
- `summary_panel.one_liner`: should sound human, concise, and easy to repeat.
- `summary_panel.top_3_problems`: short, ranked, and distinct from one another.
- `issue_cards[*].title`: should read like an observation, not a taxonomy label.
- `issue_cards[*].problem`: must connect the issue to something actually visible on the page.
- `issue_cards[*].why_it_hurts`: explain the user consequence in plain language.
- `issue_cards[*].fix`: tell the user exactly what to change next.
- `issue_cards[*].example_rewrite`: write stronger copy, not funnier copy.
- `share_card_copy.quote`: this may be the most playful line, but it still must reflect the page's real problem.

Preferred formula for each major issue
- Evidence -> insight -> sharp line -> fix
- The title carries most of the style.
- The body earns trust.
- The fix earns the product.
